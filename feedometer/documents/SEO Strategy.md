## 1. Executive Summary & Strategy Overview

FeedOmeter's SEO roadmap is built on a **6-Tier Search Intent Architecture** designed to capture users across the entire search spectrum: from high-volume discovery keywords down to high-converting competitor alternative searches and low-competition long-tail category queries.

### Core Value Propositions (USPs) for Search Copy:
- **Zero Login Required:** Instant browser-based reading with zero friction, sign-up, or tracking.
- **Universal Edge Parsing:** Seamless support for RSS 0.91/0.92/2.0, Atom 1.0, RDF, and XML.
- **Web-to-RSS Semantic Generator:** Built-in ability to turn any publication into an RSS feed.
- **Executive Card Layout:** Modern, high-density briefing cards with dark/light themes and instant preview.

## 5. Legal & Trademark Compliance Analysis

### 5.1 Generic & Standard Terms (100% Legal & Public Domain)
- **Keywords:** `RSS reader`, `XML feed viewer`, `Atom feed reader`, `News aggregator`, `JSON Feed reader`, `AI RSS reader`, `Free RSS viewer`, etc.
- **Legal Status:** **Completely safe and unrestricted.**
- **Reasoning:** RSS, Atom, XML, and JSON are open internet standards (RFCs, W3C standards). Descriptive terms such as "news aggregator", "feed reader", or "dashboard" are generic English terms that cannot be trademarked or monopolized.

### 5.2 Competitor Brand Names (`Feedly alternative`, `Inoreader alternative`, `NewsBlur alternative`, `RSS.app alternative`, `Feedbin alternative`)
- **Legal Status:** **100% Legal under the doctrine of "Nominative Fair Use"** (US Lanham Act § 33(b)(4), EU Trademark Directive, and international IP precedent).
- **Core Principle:** Trademark law explicitly permits referring to a competitor's brand name in truthful commercial comparisons and search metadata to inform consumers of alternatives.
- **Compliance Rules:**
  1. **No False Affiliation:** Clearly identify FeedOmeter as an independent application. Do not claim official partnership, endorsement, or sponsorship by Feedly/Inoreader.
  2. **Text-Only Usage:** Use standard text mentions in metadata, articles, and comparison tables. Do not display competitor corporate logos without a license.
  3. **Truthful & Factual:** Ensure all feature comparisons remain objective and accurate (e.g., "FeedOmeter requires zero account creation, whereas Feedly requires user registration").

### 5.3 Publisher & Media Brand Names (`BBC`, `CNN`, `NPR`, `Wired`, `MIT`, `Forbes`)
- **Legal Status:** **100% Legal under Syndication & Fair Use.**
- **Reasoning:** These organizations distribute public RSS/Atom feeds intended for open web syndication. Referencing publisher names strictly to identify the feed source constitutes standard nominative fair use (identical to industry practices on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and Google News).
