A Feedly/Inoreader-class app is two journeys, not one: sample without an account, then own a library after login. Plans sit on top of the owner, not on first open.

The durable map is here: RSS SaaS user journey.

Guest (only the Reader)
They arrive from Google or a pasted feed URL. They open the public RSS Reader, paste a feed, skim cards, open a story, maybe copy XML. They do not follow, star, folder, or get “my home.” If this works in 30 seconds, they understand the product. If you demand signup here, they leave.

The wall (this is conversion)
They try to keep something: star, read later, follow, make a folder, open their briefing, or save a built feed. That is when you ask for Google or email. The sentence is: this is now yours, so it needs a user.

Become an owner
Account is created (u_… identity, session, empty library). Immediately they must fill it: pick 3 topic pills, follow ~8–15 catalog sources, optionally paste a blog URL or import OPML from Feedly. Then they make a few folders (Work, AI, Markets) so the stream is not one pile. Activation is “5+ follows and Home opened once,” not “registered.”

Daily loop (this is the real product)
Morning: Home briefing → live top stories → split reader for deep reading. They open, mark read, star a few, save two for later, unfollow a noisy source. When they feel gaps they search the catalog or add a custom RSS / web-to-feed. Identity settings are rare. This loop is why they stay.

Trust and power
Same stars and folders on laptop and phone. Profile, password, Google link, revoke a stolen device, theme. Later: search their library, saved queries, alerts, AI briefs, shareable collections.

Plans (later)
Free owner: follow, folders, star/save, daily read. Paid: higher source caps, faster refresh, keyword alerts, AI, team seats. They pay when they hit a limit or a “don’t miss this” job — not at signup.

Leave
Mute, unfollow dead feeds, export OPML, delete the account for real. RSS users will not stay if export/delete is fake.

If you remember one sequence for the logged-in app: Auth → pick sources → Home → read/star/save → folders → add more sources → settings → (later) plan.

Say when you want to go back to the identity/security fixes, or turn this journey into a gated screen list in the product.
