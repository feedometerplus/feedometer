One Risk
The page currently tries to do a lot.
For a first-time user:

Trending Searches
Saved Workspaces
Alerts
Entity Velocity
Topics
Search

could feel overwhelming......

I'd consider progressive disclosure:

Guest Users
	Search + Trending Topics
Logged-in Users
	Workspaces + Alerts
Power Users:
	Intelligence Widgets

My Overall Assessment:
If executed well, this doesn't compete directly with Feedly or Inoreader......
It sits in a different category :)

RSS Reader ❌
Feed Manager ❌
News Aggregator ❌
News Intelligence Platform ✅
Research Workspace ✅
Monitoring System ✅

I'd position Feedometer as "an intelligence platform built on RSS" rather than "another RSS reader....
